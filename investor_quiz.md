# Investor Profiling Quiz



### Question 1: What is your primary goal for investing in fractional real estate?
*Helps determine the type of property (e.g., commercial yield vs. residential flip).*


### Question 2: How long are you comfortable locking in your investment?
*Filters properties based on their contract expiry and liquidity.*


### Question 3: How would you describe your risk tolerance?
*Maps to the risk rating of the recommended properties.*


### Question 4: What is your initial investment capacity for your first property?
*Provides the hard numerical filter (SQL WHERE clause) before the vector similarity search.*

### Question 5: What is your current level of experience with real estate or financial investments?
*Helps tailor the complexity of the property descriptions and risk warnings provided to the user.*
